Netra Command

AI command centre for utilities and infrastructure

GridWatch reviews drone imagery for power-line faults. Bhumi-Eye compares satellite scenes of land and water. One core assigns a priority, a crew dispatch, and a voice alert.

  • GridWatch — drone imagery fault detection
  • Bhumi-Eye — satellite change detection
  • Detection → incident priority → crew dispatch + voice alert
